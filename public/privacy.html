Privacy Policy

Last updated: [date]

Trivia Conquer doesn't use accounts, logins, or passwords. This page explains the small amount of data the app does handle.

What's stored on your device

  • An optional nickname you choose, if you submit a score to the daily leaderboard.
  • Your personal best streak, so the app can tell you when you beat it.

Both are stored locally in your browser (localStorage) and are never sent anywhere except as described below.

Daily leaderboard

If you choose to submit a score, your nickname, streak, and a timestamp are stored on the server and shown publicly on that day's leaderboard to all visitors. Entries are automatically removed after 2 days. Don't use a nickname that identifies you if you'd rather not be publicly visible.

Challenge links

The "Challenge a Friend" feature encodes a set of trivia questions and your score directly into the link itself — nothing is stored on a server for this feature, and no data about who you send it to is collected.

Trivia questions

Questions are fetched live from the third-party Open Trivia Database. No personal data is sent to it beyond a random, anonymous session token used to avoid repeating questions.

Advertising

This site may show ads served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other sites. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
